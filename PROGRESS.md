## Quality gate

A design is not final merely because the SVG exists:
1. visual/thumbnail review;
2. SVG structure review;
3. PNG 4500×5400 transparent render;
4. print-safety review;
5. originality/IP review;
6. mockup using actual final asset;
7. final human-style acceptance.

* PNG pipeline is operational; changed/new masters still require regeneration and validation.

No artificial 100% claims.
