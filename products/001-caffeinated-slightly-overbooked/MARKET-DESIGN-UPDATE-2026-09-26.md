# Market / Design Update — 2026-09-26

## Fresh research pass

This pass was completed before the next design QA stage.

### Etsy
Etsy Marketplace Insights is the preferred source for current keyword decisions because it exposes direct Etsy search data, related terms, and recent trend information. Etsy is also testing 30-day, 90-day and 12-month views, purchased price ranges, search conversion rate and week-over-week movement.

Source:
https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35122361353239-How-Do-I-Use-Etsy-s-Marketplace-Insights-Tool

### T-shirt design direction
Current 2026 guidance from Printful continues to emphasize:
- humor that is understandable at first glance;
- bold/readable typography;
- short messages;
- art + text combinations;
- limited palettes;
- intentional font pairing.

Sources:
https://www.printful.com/uk/blog/design-trends

### Design decisions for Product 001
The existing 20-design collection will be reviewed against these signals, but market trends are used as direction rather than as permission to copy existing listings.

Priority checks:
1. 1–2 second message comprehension.
2. Strong thumbnail silhouette.
3. Distinct visual hook.
4. Typography remains readable at marketplace thumbnail size.
5. Maximum two typeface roles per design where practical.
6. Avoid excessive micro-copy.
7. Avoid repetitive dashboard/status compositions across the collection.
8. Keep coffee as a theme, not the visual solution for every design.
9. Preserve original artwork and composition.
10. Reject or revise designs that look interchangeable with other bundle items.

### Marketplace listing implication
Etsy currently recommends high-resolution primary listing imagery and a singular finished-product image as the primary photo. Therefore mockups will be built from the actual final assets rather than generic placeholder artwork.
