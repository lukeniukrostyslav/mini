# Product 001 — Design 02 QA Brief

## Commercial role
Design 02 expands the collection with a cleaner, more minimal composition while keeping the coffee + calendar visual language.

## Differentiation
- calendar is used as the central illustration rather than a generic coffee mug alone;
- coffee steam forms the visual bridge between the two ideas;
- large stacked typography is optimized for apparel readability;
- no logos, characters, celebrity likenesses, or competitor artwork.
