# Product 001 — Design 06 QA Brief

## Concept
**My Calendar Needs Coffee Too**

## Commercial role
A visual joke where the calendar itself gets a coffee cup, creating a stronger image/text relationship than a slogan alone.

## Visual direction
- oversized headline;
- original calendar grid illustration;
- integrated coffee cup and steam;
- one-color production;
- designed for quick recognition at thumbnail size.
