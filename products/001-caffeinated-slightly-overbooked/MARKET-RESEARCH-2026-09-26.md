# Market & Design Research — Product 001
Date: 2026-09-26

## Current market signals

Fresh research was checked before the next production pass.

### Etsy
Etsy's Marketplace Insights provides direct Etsy buyer search data, related search terms, listing counts, and recent trend data. Etsy is also testing longer trend windows, purchased-price ranges, search conversion rate, and week-over-week movement. These are the preferred sources for future keyword/listing decisions rather than guessing from generic trend articles.

Sources:
- https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35122361353239-How-Do-I-Use-Etsy-s-Marketplace-Insights-Tool
- https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/10603291042967-Newly-Crafted-Etsy-Updates-for-Your-Shop
- https://www.etsy.com/hk-en/seller-handbook/article/382774281517

### Current competitive environment
Etsy's current funny-typography T-shirt category is highly saturated. Search results include very large bundles priced around the low single digits, so a 20-design bundle cannot compete by file count alone.

Implication:
- compete on curation, visual coherence, originality, typography quality, production readiness and presentation;
- do not copy the visual language or phrases of individual competitors;
- avoid pretending that marketplace volume equals demand for a specific design.

### Etsy marketplace context
Etsy reported 86.5 million active buyers and 5.6 million active sellers at the end of 2025; apparel is one of its major GMS-generating categories. This is marketplace context, not a sales forecast for Product 001.

## Design direction for the next pass

The collection must satisfy:
1. instant message recognition;
2. a distinct visual hook;
3. strong mobile thumbnail silhouette;
4. readable typography at small size;
5. limited, intentional visual complexity;
6. clear differentiation between designs;
7. original artwork/composition;
8. no unverified protected phrase or brand reference;
9. reproducible POD geometry;
10. professional bundle-level consistency.

## Collection strategy

The 20 designs should not become 20 variations of the same coffee/status graphic.

Target mix:
- coffee/caffeine: 5–6
- office/work: 4–5
- calendar/productivity: 3–4
- broader adult-life/relatable humor: 4–5

Coffee is the entry theme, not the entire visual identity.

## Commercial positioning

Working hypothesis:
- 20 curated original designs;
- transparent PNG at 4500×5400;
- SVG masters;
- buyer instructions;
- professional preview/mockup assets;
- clear license;
- ZIP package;
- target bundle price: approximately $6–7.

This is positioning, not a guaranteed sales outcome.

## Research rule

Before any future design is created or materially revised, perform a fresh market/phrase review. Market evidence informs the design; it does not justify copying a competitor.
