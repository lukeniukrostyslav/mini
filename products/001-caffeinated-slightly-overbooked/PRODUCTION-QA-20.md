# Product 001 — Production QA Matrix

This document prevents a master SVG from being treated as a finished commercial asset.

## Required gates for every design

1. Master SVG structure
2. 4500×5400 transparent PNG
3. PNG dimensions and alpha validation
4. Thumbnail readability
5. Print-safe geometry
6. Visual differentiation inside the collection
7. Originality/IP review
8. Actual-asset mockup
9. Listing image QA
10. Final human-style acceptance

## Market QA principle

Current Etsy tooling exposes search/trend signals including extended date ranges, price ranges, search conversion rate and week-over-week trends for eligible sellers. These are research inputs, not guarantees of sales.

Current 2026 T-shirt trend guidance emphasizes humor, fast comprehension, bold/decorative typography and concise visual statements. The collection therefore must balance demand signals with differentiation rather than copy competitors.

No design receives final approval solely because an automated test passes.
