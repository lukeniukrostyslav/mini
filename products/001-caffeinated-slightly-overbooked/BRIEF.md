# Product 001 — Caffeinated & Slightly Overbooked

## Positioning
A humorous, adult-friendly coffee/work-life T-shirt graphic for people who run on caffeine while carrying too many commitments.

## Original concept
Primary phrase:
CAFFEINATED & SLIGHTLY OVERBOOKED

Visual direction:
- bold editorial typography
- compact coffee-cup line illustration
- subtle calendar/grid motif
- clean vintage-inspired composition
- works on dark and light garments

## Buyer
Adults who like coffee, work humor, office humor and understated graphic tees.

## Intended deliverables
- transparent print-ready PNG
- editable SVG vector
- buyer README
- license
- listing preview images
- release ZIP

## Pricing hypothesis
Single design: $1.99–$2.99.
Future collection: themed 10-design bundle.

Pricing is a test hypothesis, not a guaranteed market price.

## Research note
Etsy currently shows thousands of results for coffee-shirt PNGs and funny T-shirt designs, with many digital designs displayed around low-single-digit prices. The category is crowded, so differentiation must come from original artwork, clean presentation and a coherent collection rather than copying popular listings.
