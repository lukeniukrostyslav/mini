# Product 001 — Collection Direction V2

## Decision

The original working direction was coffee/work-life humor. After reviewing the first 11 concepts together, the collection is being widened to **Modern Work Life / Adulting Humor**.

Coffee remains a sub-theme, not the entire bundle.

## Target mix for the final 20

- 5–6 coffee/caffeine designs
- 4–5 office/work humor designs
- 3–4 calendar/productivity designs
- 4–5 broader adult-life/relatable humor designs

These are targets, not filler quotas. A concept is only retained if it has a distinct visual hook and passes originality, technical and commercial QA.

## Design principles

1. Every design must work as a standalone thumbnail.
2. Avoid repeating coffee cups, circular badges and the same typography stack.
3. Rotate visual systems: editorial/grid, visual metaphor, retro, minimal, illustration-led, badge/identity, scene-based and statement typography.
4. Use short, readable copy and a clear joke.
5. Use original artwork; no copied characters, logos or protected brand identity.
6. Validate phrases/IP separately before final acceptance.
7. Final PNG/mockup must come from the actual approved master.

## Market rationale

Current Etsy guidance gives sellers access to direct search data, related terms and trend signals through Marketplace Insights. Etsy also reports a focus on more relevant and differentiated inventory and says higher-quality items are increasingly contributing to higher average order value. These signals support a curated, differentiated bundle rather than competing only on raw file count.

## Next action

Before producing designs 12–20, audit designs 1–11 as a collection. Retain, revise or replace concepts based on:
- visual differentiation;
- thumbnail clarity;
- commercial distinctiveness;
- phrase/IP risk;
- collection balance.

No design receives final status merely because an SVG exists.
