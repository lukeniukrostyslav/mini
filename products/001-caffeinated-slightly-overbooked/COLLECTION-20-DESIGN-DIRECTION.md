# Product 001 — 20-Design Collection Direction

## Locked commercial target

- Collection size: **20 original T-shirt/POD designs**
- Target price: **$6–7**
- Core files: **PNG 300 DPI + SVG**
- Product must be sold as a curated collection, not as a quantity dump.
- No copied competitor compositions, phrases, logos, characters, or protected artwork.

## Current 2026 market signals

Fresh research on Etsy and Printful shows strong activity around:
- typography-led designs;
- retro/vintage and distressed treatments;
- original humor/relatability;
- oversized graphic placements;
- color-block/gradient typography;
- single-line illustration;
- nature/organic line art;
- hobby/profession communities;
- personalization and emotionally specific niches.

Etsy's Spring/Summer 2026 trend report is based on Etsy internal US signed-in buyer activity through February 10, 2026 and emphasizes personal, expressive, intentional products. Printful's current 2026 guidance emphasizes niche specificity, emotional hooks, clean execution, and avoiding generic saturated concepts.

## Collection art direction

The collection should feel like one small apparel label, not 20 unrelated marketplace graphics.

### Visual rules

1. Maximum two type families in a design.
2. Strong hierarchy: one hero message, one supporting element.
3. Generous negative space.
4. Every design must remain readable at thumbnail size.
5. Prefer bold silhouette/shape and simple reproducible line work.
6. Use restrained palettes that print reliably.
7. Use distressed texture only where it strengthens the concept.
8. Avoid generic clip-art coffee cups and generic badge layouts.
9. Create an identifiable visual hook for every design.
10. Test every design on both light and dark garment contexts when technically appropriate.

## Design production order

Do not create all 20 blindly.

1. Build a mini style system.
2. Redesign Product 001 hero.
3. Produce 3–4 visual prototypes in different directions.
4. Evaluate thumbnail readability and collection cohesion.
5. Select the strongest direction.
6. Produce the remaining designs within that system.
7. Run individual technical/IP QA.
8. Build collection preview and mockups.
9. Package and prepare marketplace listing.

## Quality gate

A design does not count toward the 20 if it is:
- a minor variation;
- visually unfinished;
- generic filler;
- unreadable at thumbnail;
- technically unsafe for print;
- too close to a competitor;
- unresolved for licensing/IP;
- missing required production files.
