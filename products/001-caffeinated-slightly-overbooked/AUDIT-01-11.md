# Product 001 — Audit of Designs 01–11

## Decision

The first 11 concepts were created under the original coffee/work-life direction. The collection is now being widened to Modern Work Life / Adulting Humor.

### Keep as coffee core candidates
- 01 Caffeinated & Slightly Overbooked
- 04 Deadline Fuel
- 07 Busy / Brewing
- 10 Espresso Emergency

### Keep as work/calendar candidates
- 03 Daily Grind Department
- 06 My Calendar Needs Coffee Too
- 11 Calendar Chaos Club

### Candidates requiring stronger differentiation during visual QA
- 02 Coffee First, Calendar Later
- 05 Powered by Coffee + Deadlines
- 08 Coffee Break Department
- 09 Morning Shift Coffee Club

These are not deleted automatically. They must compete against later concepts on thumbnail clarity, originality, visual distinction and commercial usefulness.

## New collection mix

Target final collection:
- 5–6 coffee/caffeine
- 4–5 office/work
- 3–4 calendar/productivity
- 4–5 broader adult-life/relatable humor

No quota can force a weak design into the final 20.

## New concept filter

Every new design must answer YES to:
1. Is the joke/idea understood quickly?
2. Does it have a visual hook beyond a sentence?
3. Is it visually different from the existing 11?
4. Would it still look intentional without the listing description?
5. Can it be reproduced cleanly as POD artwork?
6. Does it avoid obvious third-party IP/brand identity?
7. Does it have a clear buyer identity or relatable situation?

## Research basis

Current Etsy guidance encourages using direct search data and related terms through Marketplace Insights. Current 2026 T-shirt guidance emphasizes humor, art+text, bold typography, concise messaging, and distinct visual expression. These are inputs, not sales guarantees.
