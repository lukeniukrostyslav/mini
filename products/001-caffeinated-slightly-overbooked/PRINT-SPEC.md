# Print Specification — Product 001

- Background: transparent
- Artwork: vector master

## QA requirements
- no background rectangle

- typography remains inside safe area
- line work remains legible at reduced size
- PNG must retain transparency
- final PNG must be opened and inspected before B04/B09 can reach 100%

## Etsy packaging constraint
Etsy's current digital-listing help says instant-download listings can contain up to five files, each up to 20 MB, and filenames are limited to 70 alphanumeric characters, periods, underscores or hyphens. Final package naming will follow those constraints.
