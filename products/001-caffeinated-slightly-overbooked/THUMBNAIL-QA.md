# Product 001 — Thumbnail QA

## Purpose
The bundle must work as a commercial collection at marketplace thumbnail size, not only at full resolution.

## Review checklist for all 20
- Message readable without zooming
- Main visual hook identifiable
- No accidental visual collision between designs
- No excessive empty space
- No tiny text that disappears in thumbnail
- No composition that depends on shirt color
- Clear distinction between coffee, work, calendar and adult-life themes
- Collection has varied silhouettes and visual systems
- Weak/redundant designs are candidates for revision before final acceptance
