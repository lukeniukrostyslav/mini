# Product 001 — Concepts 12–15 V2

The collection has been widened from coffee/work humor to Modern Work Life / Adulting Humor. These concepts deliberately reduce coffee dependence.

## 12 — Meeting Could Have Been an Email
Visual: oversized inbox/email window with a meeting notification overflowing from it.
Hook: the meeting is literally rendered as an unnecessary email conversion.
System: editorial UI / tech-office graphic.
Coffee: none.

## 13 — Out of Office, In My Head
Visual: split-state office status panel with OUT OF OFFICE selected while a chaotic thought cloud occupies the other side.
Hook: workplace status language applied to mental daydreaming without medical/mental-health claims.
System: minimal status UI.
Coffee: none.

## 14 — Weekend Loading
Visual: retro progress bar / loading screen at 97%, with Friday-to-weekend transition.
Hook: universally recognizable digital metaphor.
System: Y2K/retro tech.
Coffee: none.

## 15 — Do Not Disturb: I'm Recharging
Visual: physical battery icon integrated with a human-style recharge station / simple cable motif, no device brand.
Hook: adult social-energy humor.
System: bold minimal iconography.
Coffee: none.

## Production rule

These are concept masters only. Before final artwork:
- check phrase/IP risk;
- compare against current marketplace results;
- build a distinct visual hook;
- test thumbnail readability;
- avoid copying interface/brand identity;
- keep each design materially different from designs 01–11.

No design is final until all production gates pass.
