## Product implication

Product 001 must therefore compete through:
- curation rather than raw file count;
- original visual systems;
- strong first-glance readability;
- distinct concepts across the 20 designs;
- professional mockups;
- clean packaging and licensing;
- current, clear marketplace metadata.

## Hard design gate

No design is accepted solely because its SVG is valid or its PNG renders correctly.

Each design must also pass:
- thumbnail readability;
- visual hook;
- composition;
- collection differentiation;
- print safety;
- originality/IP review;
- actual-asset mockup review.

## Next action

Proceed with collection-wide thumbnail/visual QA and revise weak or repetitive designs before packaging.

No sales forecast is inferred from this research.
